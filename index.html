⚄ FARKLE DICE ⚀


Rules

Roll six dice. Tap scoring dice to set them aside, then Roll the rest or Bank your points. If a roll has no scoring dice, you bust and lose the turn's points. Scored all six? Hot dice – roll all six again. First to reach the target triggers a final round; highest score wins.
